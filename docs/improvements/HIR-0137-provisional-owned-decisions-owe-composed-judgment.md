# Provisional owned decisions owe composed judgment

## Observed failure

Room 1046 Layer 2 run `20260830T062901Z-901783` produced two rectangular massing
blocks that passed all six `building_mass` contracts. The f39 Workbench plate was a tiny
generic pawn silhouette; it did not resemble the reference's corner-view historic hotel.
The comparison correctly reported zero bound image checks and explicitly said that 0/0
did not certify look. The run was interrupted before the structural floor could freeze.

The selected requirement register contained the exact missing authority. R51 originally
deferred to Layer 2 with AND evidence domains `image` and `scene`, and stated that the
opening must read as a specific isolated historic hotel rather than a generic modern
tower. Materialization resolved it with an `approved_start` decision saying that the
holistic read was not reducible to one metric. Runtime treated that provisional start as
already closed and the all-executable composition would have skipped raster judgment.

## Root cause

Required image debts correctly keep missing candidate-sensitive contracts open, but a
typed requirement decision bypassed that path. `approved_start` and `planner_start` are
provisional by definition; they authorize a bounded hypothesis, not acceptance. The
composed-layer judge compiled only unit claim fan-in and ignored provisional decisions
owned by the layer.

## Decision

- A requirement originally deferred to this layer and resolved as `approved_start` or
  `planner_start` becomes mandatory composed-layer judgment debt.
- The debt is derived by exact requirement id from the immutable base bundle and selected
  overlay. No requirement prose, axis keyword, role name, or shot identity is inferred.
- The independent critic receives the exact decision statement as a qualified claim at
  every declared layer judge frame, after all bounded unit scripts replay cumulatively.
- A composition with no look capabilities renders Workbench solid through the sealed
  camera. A look-owning composition continues to render EEVEE. Form is not forced to
  manufacture light or shading signal.
- Failure is a `contract_gap` for transactional replanning. The synthetic composition
  judge grants no cross-unit mutation authority and canonical repair cannot edit several
  unit artifacts as one broad layer.
- `hard_constraint` is authored truth and `confirmed_outcome` is already evidence-pinned;
  neither creates this provisional audit.

## General mechanism

`_provisional_decisions_for_layer` joins base deferred ownership to selected provisional
decision rows. `_composition_judge_unit` compiles those exact records into qualified
claims alongside the ordinary cumulative claim fan-in. `_unit_raster_mode` selects solid
for look-less qualitative evidence and EEVEE only where typed look capabilities exist.

The critic sees the synthetic active-unit claim manifest directly. A failed provisional
audit is rewritten as an uncovered authority boundary and persisted by the existing
contract-gap machinery, so the next legal operation is replan rather than speculative
multi-unit repair.

## Rejected alternatives

- Add looser bbox or vertex-count bands: the observed generic block already passed them;
  no scalar count proves historic-hotel identity.
- Trust the builder's terminal description: nothing self-certifies.
- Force a beauty/image-contract debt on geometry: HIR-0110 correctly rejects optical
  self-certification without a light/shading/volume/compositor signal producer.
- Let the composition critic edit all roles: that destroys bounded unit ownership and
  replay identity.
- Special-case R51, frame 39, or hotel/tower keywords: the mechanism keys only on typed
  ownership and decision strength.

## Validation

Focused fixtures prove that an owned `approved_start` compiles to a qualified composition
claim carrying the exact statement, requires raster, and selects Workbench solid when the
constituent units own no look. Existing executable-only compositions without provisional
decisions still skip raster and critic judgment.

Production validation requires completing bounded Layer 2 units, then observing the
composed f39 audit reject a generic proxy or accept a reference-specific historic hotel.

Validation on 2026-08-30: focused staged-architecture, builder-instrument, and visibility
suites passed 68 tests; the full repository suite passed 633 tests in 46.91 seconds; and
`.venv/bin/ruff check src tests` passed.

## Release and rollback

No persisted schema change. Rollback lets provisional holistic decisions disappear at
materialization and allows structural floors to self-certify reference identity.
