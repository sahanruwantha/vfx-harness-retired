# Short durable decision

## Context

Describe the forces, constraints, and linked HIR or research evidence that require a durable
choice.

## Decision

State the chosen architecture, authority, contract, boundary, or policy precisely. Include the
scope and what this decision does not authorize.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, migration effects, operational impact, and new risks.

## Rejected alternatives

Record viable alternatives and the criteria by which they lost. Do not omit an alternative only
because it was not implemented.

## Validation and review trigger

Link the evidence that makes this decision safe enough to accept. State what future evidence or
condition should cause it to be reviewed, superseded, or reversed.
